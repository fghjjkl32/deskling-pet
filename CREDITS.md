# Credits

## Bundled characters

The Boy and Girl characters were designed by the project author, fghjjkl32; the original drawings are `art/boy/original.png` and `art/girl/original.png`.

The pose sheets in `art/boy/` and `art/girl/` were generated with Google Gemini from those drawings, then turned into animated PNG files with `scripts/sheet-to-skin.py`.
