# Credits

## Bundled characters

The Boy and Girl characters were designed by the project author, fghjjkl32. The source drawings are in `art/`.

- **Boy**: the pose sheets in `art/boy/` were generated with an image model from the author's drawing `art/boy/original.png`, then turned into animated PNG files with `scripts/sheet-to-skin.py`.
- **Girl**: every pose is generated from the single drawing `art/girl/original.png` with `scripts/make-skin.py`, which moves, tilts and squashes it.
